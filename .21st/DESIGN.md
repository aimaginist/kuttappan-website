# Kuttappan Design Context

## Direction

Monsoon Matinee is the master direction: a cinematic Malayalam comedy experience built from full-bleed film scenes, numbered editorial chapters, approved character sheets and restrained film-credit typography.

## Visual System

- Palette: ink, rice paper, laterite, backwater teal, monsoon green, harvest gold and sky blue.
- Type: Bodoni Moda for display, DM Sans for interface copy, Manjari for Malayalam.
- Motion: staged reveals, parallax, scene scaling, a desktop pinned film reel and a three-film hero sequence.
- Shape: 4px controls and cards; visual hierarchy comes from composition, borders and contrast rather than decorative shadows.

## Non-negotiables

- Use supplied films and approved master sheets only.
- Keep all character canon exact.
- Do not invent Malayalam or lore.
- Keep Shorts out of the main episode grid.
- Keep YouTube click-to-play and honor reduced motion.
